# Design Influences 2026 — Drenyra Web

**Última actualización:** 2026-07-24  
**Scope:** `apps/web` — Drenyra Financial Engineering Environment

## Purpose

Document external design references that inform Drenyra's **Financial Engineering Environment** command center without copying them literally. Product remains **operations-first** for Peruvian fiscal compliance with FSD/RED rigor.

> FEE principle: design for focus, evidence visibility, and review clarity — not for chat-like minimalism or decorative UI.

---

## Fiscal context (non-negotiable)

Peruvian operators need: RUC scoping, SUNAT states, IGV clarity, long-session readability, audit trails, receipt-driven evidence, and specification-driven workflows (FSD). Any influence that reduces evidence visibility or fiscal correctness is rejected regardless of trend.
