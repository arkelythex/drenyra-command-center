# Security Policy

**Last updated**: 2026-05-31

---

*Alineado con la [Filosofía Gentleman](docs/meta/gentleman-philosophy.md) de ARKELYTHEX — documentación clara, progresiva y que trata cada lector como un colega.*

## Supported scope

This repository is treated as proprietary Arkelythex infrastructure. Security reports may cover source code, deployment configuration, documentation, CI, authentication, data handling, tenant isolation, SUNAT/compliance flows, and exposed secrets.

## Reporting vulnerabilities

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the Arkelythex legal/security contact. Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly before Arkelythex has confirmed remediation or provided written authorization.

Include:

- Affected component or path.
- Reproduction steps.
- Impact assessment.
- Logs, screenshots, or proof-of-concept details with no real customer data.

## Prohibited in reports

- Real credentials, tokens, or private keys.
- Customer, prospect, or taxpayer data.
- Destructive testing against production systems.
- Public disclosure before coordinated remediation.

## Internal handling

Security findings must be tracked with severity, owner, remediation status, verification evidence, and closure date. Fiscal correctness, tenant isolation, RUC/company scoping, and auditability are treated as product safety requirements.
