# Vision

Build an open-source civic-tech platform for Peru focused on:
1) Radical transparency (auditable, verifiable)
2) Citizen participation (structured, scalable)
3) Public-service usability (frictionless, inclusive)

## Non-goals
- Not a political party
- Not a propaganda platform
- Not "AI decides policy"

AI is used as tooling (search, summarization, triage), while decisions remain accountable.

## Success criteria
- A municipal MVP replicated in multiple regions
- Measurable impact (time saved, corruption signals surfaced, participation increased)
- Trust: public audit trails, clear governance, privacy compliance
