# On-Call v1

## Coverage model
- Business-hours on-call baseline for MVP stage.
- Escalation to Project Lead for Sev-1 incidents.

## Rotation
- Weekly primary and secondary assignees.
- Rotation schedule maintained in maintainers board.

## On-call responsibilities
- Acknowledge alerts.
- Triage severity.
- Trigger incident process for Sev-1/2.
- Ensure post-incident follow-up tasks are tracked.

## Handover checklist
- Open incidents and owner.
- High-burn SLO alerts.
- Deferred mitigations and due dates.
